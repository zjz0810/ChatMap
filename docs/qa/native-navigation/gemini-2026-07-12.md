## 2026-07-13 Native-index adaptation

- Implementation: a clean-room `document_start` MAIN-world observer passively captures Gemini's `hNvQHb` conversation response. The content adapter builds stable conversation/request/response identities without scrolling and prefers that complete native index over mounted-DOM extraction.
- Deterministic binding: mounted turns are bound only by a complete ordered match. This keeps repeated prompts distinct across remounts and refuses partial or ambiguous bindings instead of guessing by text similarity.
- Navigation safety: direct navigation reveals an already mounted, deterministically bound user turn. An unmounted or unresolved target returns an explicit failure and does not trigger extraction scrolling or text search.
- Automated evidence: synthetic protocol fixtures cover full indexing, repeated response branches, streaming enrichment, remounts, SPA conversation isolation, appended DOM tails, incomplete-binding safe failure, adapter routing, and eager observer capture.
- Browser probe: the unpacked build loaded on a real anonymous Gemini page under Playwright Chromium; both the early observer and exactly one TurnMap launcher were present. The anonymous composer could not be driven reliably enough to produce a new conversation during this run, so no fresh `hNvQHb` payload or 20-turn/off-screen acceptance evidence was recorded.
- Capability decision: retain `smoke-verified` at the mounted-DOM tier. The native path is implemented and evidence-gated, but full native indexing, distant off-screen jump, and shell remount are not promoted until real long-conversation evidence exists.
